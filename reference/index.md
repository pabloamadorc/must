# Package index

## Workflow

The steps of an analysis, in order: describe the design, fit the
responses, choose the null models, estimate and classify the
interactions, compare the null models and report the methods.

- [`describe_design()`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/describe_design.md)
  : Describe what a multiple-stressor design can identify
- [`fit_responses()`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/fit_responses.md)
  : Fit a GLMM to each response, choosing its error distribution
- [`suggest_null_models()`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/suggest_null_models.md)
  : Suggest null models from what is known about the stressors
- [`estimate_interactions()`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/estimate_interactions.md)
  : Estimate single, combined and interaction effects under null models
- [`summary(`*`<must_interactions>`*`)`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/must_interactions-methods.md)
  [`plot(`*`<must_interactions>`*`)`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/must_interactions-methods.md)
  : Summarise and plot estimated interactions
- [`classify_interactions()`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/classify_interactions.md)
  : Classify interactions (Piggott et al. 2015)
- [`compare_null_models()`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/compare_null_models.md)
  : Agreement between null models on each interaction
- [`report_interactions()`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/report_interactions.md)
  : Report the methods and the decisions of an analysis

## Data: case studies

Six published experiments with other designs, reanalysed in the case
studies.

- [`alba_arthropods`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/alba_arthropods.md)
  : Arthropods in a drought and plant invasion field experiment
- [`fuggle_biofilms`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/fuggle_biofilms.md)
  : Biofilm metabolism under salinisation and nutrient enrichment in two
  orders
- [`giesler_diatoms`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/giesler_diatoms.md)
  : Growth of two diatoms under temperature, photoperiod and microbiome
  removal
- [`hunn_invertebrates`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/hunn_invertebrates.md)
  : Stream invertebrates under CO2 enrichment, heatwaves, flow
  variability and fine sediment
- [`niboyet_denitrification`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/niboyet_denitrification.md)
  : Potential denitrification under five global changes
- [`vasquez_embryos`](https://pabloamadorc.github.io/must/reference/vasquez_embryos.md)
  : Development of horseshoe crab embryos under temperature, salinity
  and oxygen
